# BrevityRx Landing

Build a single-page marketing landing site for BrevityRx, an autonomous orchestration platform that automates specialty medication prior authorization for specialty pharmacies and health systems. The platform uses agentic AI to bridge the gap between EMR data and payor medical necessity rules, reducing therapy delays from weeks to under 48 hours.

Design direction: Clean, modern clinical aesthetic — trustworthy and professional like healthcare software, but polished and contemporary. Use the following colors:
$sky-surge: #5bc0ebff;

$baltic-blue: #235789ff;

$white: #ffffffff;

$dark-amaranth: #7d1d3fff;

$blush-rose: #e36588ff;

White/light backgrounds, generous whitespace, crisp typography (a sans-serif like Inter or DM Sans). Subtle gradients and soft shadows. Generate a simple creative, unique, invokes empathy logo for "BrevityRx" that feels precise and medical yet approachable.

Page structure (single scrolling page):

Hero section — Bold headline communicating the core value prop: right clinical proof reaches the insurer the first time, eliminating PA delays. Subheadline emphasizing "weeks to under 48 hours." Primary CTA: "Join the Waitlist" email capture form. Optional subtle animation or abstract illustration suggesting automation/workflow.

Problem statement — Brief section framing the pain: specialty pharmacies lose revenue and patients experience dangerous therapy delays because prior auth is manual, error-prone, and slow. Use a compelling stat placeholder (e.g., "X% of specialty PAs are initially denied due to incomplete documentation").

How it works — Three-step visual flow: (1) Connect to EMR data, (2) AI maps clinical evidence to payor-specific rules, (3) Complete, compliant PA submitted automatically. Use icons or simple illustrations for each step.

Key benefits — Three to four cards highlighting outcomes: faster approvals (under 48 hours), recovered revenue, reduced staff burden, improved patient outcomes. Use placeholder metrics with clean iconography.

Who it's for — Brief section addressing two personas: specialty pharmacies and health systems. One or two sentences each on how BrevityRx helps them specifically.

Social proof placeholder — Section ready for future logos, testimonials, or case study snippets. For now, use a clean "Trusted by leading specialty pharmacies" placeholder with greyed-out logo spots.

Final CTA — Repeated waitlist signup with a line like "Be the first to eliminate the prior auth bottleneck."

Footer — Company name, copyright, placeholder links for Privacy Policy, Terms, and Contact.

Technical notes: Fully responsive (mobile-first). Smooth scroll navigation. Email capture form should store submissions (use Supabase or similar). Keep the page fast and lightweight. No authentication needed for this version.
